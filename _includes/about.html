About

I’m a second-year electrical engineering student at Northeastern University in Boston. I’m drawn to hardware that sits between the analog and digital worlds: picking up a real signal, conditioning it, and making sense of it in firmware.

Outside of class I work on the battery management system for Northeastern Electric Racing, and I’ve converted a 1977 Volkswagen Bus to electric drive. I’m looking for a Spring 2027 co-op in electronic design or embedded systems.

Analog design

Signal-chain circuits such as microphone preamps and ADC front ends, designed in schematic and built on the bench.

Embedded systems

C++ firmware for ESP32 and Arduino boards, from capturing data to processing it on the device.

Power and vehicles

Battery management for a Formula-style race car and a complete electric powertrain conversion.

Tools I use: KiCad, Altium Designer, C++, Arduino, ESP32, Git
